## Why a follow-up phase, not a patch

User reviewed two side-by-side options on device after the constraint-timing fix landed:
1. **Smallest** — drop VERIFIED text inside the chip (compact-mode badge), keep the 2D graph.
2. **Medium** — chip refactor with avatar + dot, keep the 2D graph topology.
3. **Mockup first** — sketch then choose.
4. **Big** — adopt the reference design's vertical "Chain of vouches" tree pattern.

User chose **option 4**: the 2D trust-graph visualization is not the right primitive for the chain-of-trust marquee at iPhone widths. A vertical attribution tree is more readable and exposes the *vouching* relationship explicitly via "VIA SHP" / "VIA BRK" attribution labels.

## Reference design (from user's second photo, 2026-05-20)

The reference has two stacked surfaces in the Load Detail body:

**Top — "Everyone on this load" horizontal strip**
- Heading: "Everyone on this load" with right-side meta caption "All watching live · tap for details"
- Strip of 5 role chips arranged horizontally: SHP (Shipper) / BRK (Broker) / CAR (Carrier) / DSP (Dispatch) / FCT (Factoring).
- Each chip is a colored avatar circle (per-role brand color: blue/orange/green/purple/blue) with a 3-letter abbreviation INSIDE the circle, and a SMALL green dot at the bottom-right of the circle indicating "verified live" status. Role name appears as a caption below each avatar.
- One avatar is selectable (rendered with a darker tinted background panel — in the photo it's BRK/Broker that's selected). Tapping a chip selects that role in the tree below.

**Below — "Chain of vouches" card**
- Card title: "Chain of vouches" with right-side meta caption "Every party attested".
- Subtitle: "Every party on this load is here because someone above them attested to them. Tap a chip to see the vouch."
- Vertical attribution tree:
  - SHP / Shipper / ROOT  (no attribution suffix — root of trust)
  - BRK / Broker / VIA SHP  (currently selected — rendered with a green outline/border)
  - CAR / Carrier / VIA BRK
  - DSP / Dispatch / VIA CAR
  - FCT / Factoring / VIA CAR  (DSP and FCT are siblings, both children of CAR; rendered as two side-by-side pills below CAR)
- Each row is a horizontal pill containing: avatar circle (left) + role name + VIA attribution suffix.
- Dashed vertical lines connect parents to children; CAR → DSP/FCT uses a Y-fork dashed line.
- Card footer: green-tinted pill "✓ Chain intact · 4 vouches, no anonymous parties" — replaces the current standalone ChainIntegrityBannerView.

**Below the card** — Load details, cargo (Packaged berries · Frozen · keep at or below 0 °F), agreed rate ($3,420.00), Bill of lading number, ETA.

## Diagnosed problems in the current Phase 9 implementation

1. **Chip horizontal budget is over-subscribed.** TrustNodeView composes role caption + Phase 8 VerificationBadgeView pill (icon + text) + 2-line displayName label inside a chrome that's ~70-80pt wide at iPhone slot positions. The pill alone eats ~50pt; displayName tail-truncates to 1 character.
2. **2D positioning doesn't scale to multi-broker / multi-factoring chains.** The role-slot table in TrustGraphView has one slot per Role. VL-1009 has 2 brokers (`freightwise` + `keystone`); they collide at the same slot even after the constraint-timing fix. Already noted as a known follow-up in `phase-9-execution-closeout` memory.
3. **The "attestation" relationship is implicit.** Arrows between nodes communicate topology but not provenance — who vouched for whom. The reference's "VIA SHP" / "VIA BRK" suffix makes the chain-of-trust ledger explicit.
4. **VerificationBadgeView is a chip-unfriendly primitive.** Its TRUST-02 lock is correct as a single source of truth for the color ramp, but the pill+text format only works on roomier surfaces (load list cells, detail headers). A compact icon-only mode is needed for chip contexts; the reference goes further and uses a tiny status dot on the avatar.

## Proposed scope (subject to spec-phase refinement)

- **Replace `TrustGraphView` with two new components:**
  - `EveryoneOnLoadStripView` — horizontal scrollable strip of role-avatar chips. Tap selects a role and scrolls the vouches tree to highlight it.
  - `ChainOfVouchesView` — vertical attribution tree with parent→child dashed connectors and the "VIA X" attribution suffix. Replaces the 2D graph entirely.
- **Add a new compact-mode primitive for VerificationBadgeView** OR **introduce a sibling `RoleAvatarView`** that owns the avatar+dot pattern (per-role brand colors, status-dot color from VerificationState). Decision deferred to spec-phase: extending the existing badge keeps TRUST-02 lock simple; new component is cleaner but doubles maintenance surface.
- **Fold `ChainIntegrityBannerView` into the new card's footer** — the green/red "Chain intact / Chain compromised" pill becomes part of the ChainOfVouchesView card instead of a separate top-of-screen banner. Reduces vertical real estate consumption.
- **Migrate accessibility traversal** — the current Phase 9 D-22 accessibility container that groups TrustGraphView nodes into a single VoiceOver group with composed labels needs to be re-modeled around the new tree structure. Each vouch-tree row becomes a leaf with a composed label like "Broker FreightWise Brokerage, vouched for by Shipper Greenfield Produce, verified."
- **Multi-role-sharing chain support.** VL-1009's two brokers (freightwise + keystone) get two rows under the BRK section of the tree, both with `VIA SHP` attribution. Solves the role-slot collision known follow-up.

## Out of scope (initial cut — confirm in spec-phase)

- iPad split-view layout — the current Phase 9 iPad split is a 2-pane composition with the graph in the right pane. The new vertical tree may fit either pane; design decision deferred.
- "Live · tap for details" presence indicators — the reference shows "All watching live" status. We don't currently have a presence/realtime substrate; this may be a milestone-3 feature, not 9.1.
- Animation/transition between the strip selection and the tree highlight — design polish, deferable.

## Open questions to resolve during /gsd-spec-phase 9.1

- **Keep or remove 2D graph?** Pure replacement, or keep the 2D graph behind a debug toggle (mirroring the `-MockKYCStatusVerified` pattern just established) for animations/screenshots/marketing?
- **Compact-badge vs. new RoleAvatarView?** TRUST-02 lock implication.
- **"Everyone on this load" — is it just a re-presentation of the same chain data, or does it need a different data shape?** (The reference shows 5 roles always; what if a chain has no Factoring party?)
- **Multi-broker / multi-factoring chain semantics.** Are sibling-role nodes ordered? Is their voucher always the same as the single-role parent (e.g. both brokers VIA SHP), or can they have different vouchers?
- **Where does the new layout sit in the iPhone scroll order relative to the load summary / cargo / rate / BOL number?** The reference shows the chain BELOW the avatar strip and ABOVE load details — current Phase 9 puts the trust graph at a different position.
- **Migration of accessibility traversal contract (D-22) — fully rewrite the accessibility tests or preserve composed-label conventions?**
